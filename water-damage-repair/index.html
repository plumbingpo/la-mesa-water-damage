Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • A water stain should stay unpainted and unpatched while its cause is still active.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • Wood floor boards stayed raised, cupped or crowned even once the moisture was gone.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
